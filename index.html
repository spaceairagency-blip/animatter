01 / The problem

Your IDE shouldn’t need a loading bar.

Popular IDEs grew into corporate platforms: heavy, slow to start, packed with features you never open and prompts you never asked for. For a simple Python script, that is a lot of machine to move.

Typical IDE

Slow startup, gigabytes of RAM, constant popups, telemetry, a settings maze, and plugins to fix the plugins.

Antimatter

Opens instantly, stays out of your way, shows issues only when you ask, and keeps everything on your machine.
